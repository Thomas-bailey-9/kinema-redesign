# 2. Cinematic opening

(1440×900, 768×1024, 390×844, 320×640). Private "before" captures:

## Before (saved homepage snapshot, 17 Nov 2026)
- The snapshot's first screen shows a large empty grey area beside the Quick Book panel on desktop.
  That area holds a hero carousel of 10 images which is hidden in the snapshot because its script
  was removed. **This is a snapshot limitation, not a verified defect of the live site** — the live
  opening was not observed, so no claim is made about how it looks or behaves.
- No welcome message or headline was visible above the fold in the snapshot; the first readable
  headings were "Quick Book" and "Subscribe to our newsletter", with "What's On" below.
- Evidence category: snapshot observation (not verified against the live site).

## Redesign
- A venue-led opening: a real dusk photograph of The Kinema in the Woods (CC BY-SA 2.0, credited
  on the page) beneath the Section 1 glass header.
- Headline across three deliberate lines: "A little woodland. / A little wonder. / A world of
  cinema." with the line "Make an evening of it. Discover your next film at The Kinema in the
  Woods."
- Two actions: "Explore what's on" (primary, champagne) and "Discover the Kinema" (glass), leading
  to the What's On and Our Story placeholders until those sections exist.
- A compact featured-film glass panel, labelled "Featured film · Demo", with original artwork
  and a fictional sample film ("The Lantern Path"); "View film" opens a labelled preview demo.
- Desktop: introduction lower-left, panel lower-right, the neon sign and main gable left clear.
  Phone/tablet: the photograph becomes a top band cropped to the entrance, the introduction
  overlaps its darker foreground, and the panel follows beneath at natural height.

## What changed
- From a booking widget and promotions above the fold to a welcoming introduction with a clear
  primary route into the programme.
- The venue itself becomes the opening image, instead of a rotating carousel.
- A single featured film replaces multiple competing promotional panels in the first view.
- New concept: the featured-film panel and its preview have no captured original equivalent.

## Design reasoning (approved choices)
- **Imagery:** the real building at dusk — warm cinema lights against darker woodland — makes the
  "cinema in the woods" identity immediate and authentic rather than generic.
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond for the headline gives an elegant, cinematic voice; Inter keeps
  supporting copy and buttons plain and readable. Three short lines create rhythm and pause.
- **Colour and contrast:** restrained forest/charcoal gradients — a deeper dusk sky behind the
  header, a left-hand field for the text and a darker foreground — keep warm lights visible while
  text measures ≥ 6.8:1 (header) and ≥ 10:1 (headline) against the lightest photo pixels.
- **Layout:** text and panel sit at the edges so the photograph stays the subject; stacked on small
  screens with natural height instead of squeezing into one screen.
- **Motion:** a gentle 1.03→1 image settle and a short fade-up reveal; hover lift, glass-edge
  brightening and contained artwork zoom; reused light sweep. All content is usable immediately and
  motion is removed under reduced-motion.

## Why it helps (design intentions — not user-tested)
- Showing the venue is intended to set expectations and atmosphere from the first second.
- A single, clear primary action is intended to make finding a film the obvious next step.
- A compact featured-film panel is intended to spark interest without overwhelming the opening.
- Readable contrast, keyboard focus and touch feedback aim to keep the opening usable for everyone.

## Outstanding
- Featured film, artwork and description are fictional samples; the real programme arrives with
  What's On (Section 4) and film details (Section 5).
- Opening actions currently lead to placeholders.
